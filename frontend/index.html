Regional Municipal Road Guidelines

Mumbai MMRDA & Pune PMC Commercial Freight Regulations

Peak-Hour HCV Urban Entry Prohibition

Under Section 115/116 of the Motor Vehicles Act, heavy goods vehicles (HCVs > 7.5T GVW) are barred from entering city limits during:

  • Morning Peak: 08:00 AM – 11:30 AM
  • Evening Peak: 05:00 PM – 09:30 PM
  • Exemptions: Light Commercial Delivery Vans (Tata Ace, Dost < 3.5T GVW) are allowed.
  • Penalty: Minimum fine of ₹20,000 and vehicle impoundment.

Flyover Height Clearance Barriers

Most urban flyovers feature overhead clearance gantries capped at 2.5 meters. Freight trucks exceeding this clearance must navigate ground arterial junctions, adding an average of 35% in transit delays.

Arterial Bridge GVW Limits

Heritage and coastal connecting bridges enforce a maximum Gross Vehicle Weight of 7.5 Tonnes. Heavy multi-axle freight must detour via the peripheral outer ring road corridors.
