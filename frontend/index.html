Mumbai Regional Commercial Transport Rules

Reference guide for drivers and dispatchers

1. Heavy Commercial Vehicle Peak-Hour No-Entry

Under Section 115 and 116 of the Motor Vehicles Act, heavy goods trucks (such as Tata 407, Eicher Pro, and multi-axle trucks) are prohibited from entering urban roads during peak traffic hours:

  • Morning Window: 08:00 AM to 11:30 AM
  • Evening Window: 05:00 PM to 09:30 PM
  • Vehicles Exempt: Small delivery vans under 3.5 tonnes GVW (such as Tata Ace, Mahindra Bolero, Ashok Leyland Dost).
  • Violation Penalty: Minimum fine of Rs 20,000 and vehicle seizure.

2. Flyover Height Barrier Limits

Most urban flyovers have overhead steel gantries fixed at a height of 2.5 meters. Freight trucks exceeding 2.5 meters must take ground-level junction roads, which adds roughly 30% to travel time.

3. Bridge Weight Load Limits

Older arterial bridges restrict vehicles weighing more than 7.5 Tonnes Gross Vehicle Weight. Heavy freight must take the peripheral bypass corridors.
